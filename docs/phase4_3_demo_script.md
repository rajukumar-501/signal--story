# Phase 4.3 — Accenture Decision Intelligence: Live Demo Script

**Scenario:** S003 (China / Product `A2520150501` / Gross Sales Collapse / April 2021)  
**Target Duration:** 3 to 5 Minutes  
**Audience:** Hackathon Judges, Executive Business Leaders & Technical Evaluators  
**Application URL:** `http://127.0.0.1:8000`

---

```
[0:00] Intro Problem ──► [0:20] Anomaly ──► [0:40] Root Cause ──► [1:20] Evidence
                                                                         │
[3:20] Action ◄── [2:40] Why Competitors Lost ◄── [2:00] Arbitration ◄──┘
     │
     └──► [3:40] Trust & Governance ──► [4:20] Value & Differentiation
```

---

### [0:00 – 0:20] Introduction: The Business Problem
- **UI Action:** Open the portal at `http://127.0.0.1:8000`. Ensure `S003` is selected.
- **What Appears:** The Accenture Decision Intelligence executive dark-mode dashboard with the 4-part decision cards and meta bar.
- **What to Say:**
  > *"Traditional BI dashboards tell business leaders what happened, but they leave executives stranded when it comes to answering 'Why did it happen?' and 'What should we do next?' Today, we present the Accenture Decision Intelligence Platform — an enterprise decision system that takes complex multi-source data from anomaly detection all the way to verified executive action."*

---

### [0:20 – 0:40] Step 1: Detect the Business Anomaly
- **UI Action:** Direct the audience's attention to the top-left **Anomaly Card** in View 1.
- **What Appears:**
  - Metric: **Gross Sales Collapse**
  - Delta: **`-72.1%`** (Crimson red glow)
  - Actual vs Baseline: **$994.25** actual vs **$3,558.03** historical baseline.
- **What to Say:**
  > *"In April 2021, our telemetry detected a severe business anomaly in China: gross revenue for Product A2520150501 collapsed by -72.1% against its 3-month baseline. That is an immediate revenue drop from $3,558 to under $1,000."*

---

### [0:40 – 1:20] Step 2: Establish the Primary Root Cause
- **UI Action:** Point to the top-right **Primary Root Cause Hero Card**.
- **What Appears:**
  - Driver: **`DRIVER_03_MARKETING` (Marketing Campaign Inefficiency)**
  - Status: **`STRONGLY_SUPPORTED`** (Glowing green badge)
  - Confidence: **`HIGH CONFIDENCE`**
- **What to Say:**
  > *"Instead of generating generic chatbot speculation, our causal arbitration engine automatically identifies the true root cause: Marketing Campaign Inefficiency. The system establishes this with high confidence and Strongly Supported status."*

---

### [1:20 – 2:00] Step 3: Inspect Key Supporting Evidence
- **UI Action:** Scroll slightly to the bottom-left **Key Supporting Evidence Card Grid**.
- **What Appears:**
  - `EVD-002`: Marketing Spend surged to **1,641.07** (+40% MoM increase).
  - `EVD-003`: Conversion Rate plummeted to **3.63%** (-42% MoM drop).
- **What to Say:**
  > *"Why is the system so sure? Look at the empirical proof. In `fact_marketing_monthly`, digital ad spend expanded by +40%, but acquisition conversion rates dropped by -42%. The business was pouring money into a broken acquisition funnel."*

---

### [2:00 – 2:40] Step 4: Competing Hypotheses & Evidence Arbitration
- **UI Action:** Click Tab 2: **"Evidence & Reasoning"**.
- **What Appears:**
  - The **Candidate Arbitration Matrix** displaying all 8 investigated hypotheses.
  - The **"Why Selected"** rationale callout and **"Why Alternatives Were Rejected"** accordion.
- **What to Say:**
  > *"A key differentiator of our platform is rigorous hypothesis arbitration. The system investigated 8 competing business drivers simultaneously:*
  > *• Pricing (`DRIVER_02_PRICING`): Investigated and rejected because competitor prices remained steady.*
  > *• Product Returns (`DRIVER_04_RETURNS`): Investigated and rejected because customer return rates showed no spike.*
  > *• Inventory (`DRIVER_01_INVENTORY`): Disqualified because stock levels were healthy.*
  > *Marketing won because it had exact market scope alignment, temporal precedence, and zero contradictory data signals."*

---

### [2:40 – 3:20] Step 5: Claim-Level Grounding & Decision Action Plan
- **UI Action:** Scroll down to the **Claim-Level Grounding Stream** in View 2, click an `[EVD-002]` chip (which smoothly scrolls back to View 1 and highlights the card), then focus on the **Recommended Action Plan** in View 1.
- **What Appears:**
  - Numbered action items:
    1. *Immediate Campaign Pause:* Halt underperforming ad sets for `A2520150501` in China.
    2. *Conversion Funnel Audit:* Inspect regional ad landing pages and targeting criteria.
  - Decision Implication box showing margin protection.
- **What to Say:**
  > *"Every single sentence generated by our reasoning layer is tagged by claim type — Observation, Interpretation, Causal Conclusion, and Recommendation — and backed by verified data citations. Finally, the system tells executive leadership exactly what to do: immediately pause the underperforming ad campaign and audit regional landing pages, saving $1,641/month in wasted spend."*

---

### [3:20 – 4:00] Step 6: Trust, Safety & Governance
- **UI Action:** Click Tab 3: **"Decision Trace & Trust"**.
- **What Appears:**
  - 10-Step Deterministic Safety Validator: **`PASSED (100% Gated)`**
  - Ground-Truth Leakage: **`0 (Strictly Isolated)`**
  - Provenance Telemetry: **`LIVE_GEMINI (gemini-3.6-flash)`** / **`MOCK_PROVIDER`**
  - Secret Isolation: **`API KEY PROTECTED`**
- **What to Say:**
  > *"For compliance and auditability, View 3 demonstrates total transparency:*
  > *• 100% Evidence Grounding with 0% unsupported claims.*
  > *• 10-step deterministic safety validation guaranteeing zero hallucinations.*
  > *• Full data lineage tracking every finding back to immutable warehouse partitions.*
  > *• And complete API key isolation — credentials never leave the server."*

---

### [4:00 – 5:00] Conclusion: Business Value & Differentiation
- **UI Action:** Click **"Reset Demo"** in the top header to cleanly return to the initial state.
- **What to Say:**
  > *"In summary, the Accenture Decision Intelligence Platform bridges the critical gap between big data and executive action. It transforms noisy business anomalies into clear, grounded, and trusted decisions in seconds. Thank you."*
