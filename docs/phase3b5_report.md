## 5. Temporal Causality Validation

Verified in `tests/test_phase3b5_adversarial.py`:
- `BEFORE` indicators (lead signals, e.g. return surges preceding sales collapse) are rewarded ($+2.0$).
- `DURING` indicators receive neutral baseline attribution ($+1.0$).
- `AFTER` indicators (lagging post-event price drops or budget cuts) are penalized ($-2.0$).
- `NO_CLEAR_ALIGNMENT` triggers caution and prevents high-confidence causal attribution.

---

## 6. Scope Correctness Validation

Verified in `tests/test_phase3b5_adversarial.py`:
- Direct product-level evidence matching the target SKU is scored `EXACT` ($+3.0$).
- Out-of-scope SKU or market evidence is categorized `OUT_OF_SCOPE` ($-4.0$) and rejected.

## 7. Multi-Source Corroboration Validation

Verified in `tests/test_phase3b5_adversarial.py`:
- 10 rows from a single dataset (`fact_sales_monthly`) are counted as **1** distinct source.
- Triangulation across 2 distinct datasets (e.g. `fact_sales_monthly` + `fact_support_tickets`) is recognized as independent corroboration and rewarded ($+2.0$).

---

## 8. Contradiction Handling Validation

Verified in `tests/test_phase3b5_adversarial.py`:
- Candidates with contradictory signals (e.g. ad clicks rising $+50\%$ while sales drop) receive a $-4.0$ penalty per contradiction.
- Competing clean candidates outrank conflicted candidates even when initial heuristic scores are higher.

## 9. Uncertainty & Abstention Validation

- Missing evidence, outcome-only telemetry, global macro slowdowns, post-event evidence, and conflicting indicators all return `driver = null`, `status = "NOT_ESTABLISHED"`, and `confidence = "NONE"`.

## 10. Prompt-Injection Resistance Validation

## 11. Hallucination Resistance Validation
