Phase 3A provides the deterministic, mathematically grounded analytical foundation for the Accenture Decision Intelligence Prototype. ## 3. Evidence Roles & Taxonomy

Every evidence item generated by the analytical engine belongs to one of three roles:
1. `OUTCOME`: The target KPI anomaly under investigation (e.g. gross sales drop). Does not establish root cause.
2. `SUPPORTING`: Driver-specific indicators observed before or during the event month (e.g. stockout hours, competitor price gap, ticket volume surge, channel revenue drop).
3. `CONTRADICTORY`: Clashing indicators that logically weaken the driver hypothesis (e.g. lower prices during alleged pricing issue, stable stock during alleged inventory issue).

## 4. Candidate Generation & Scope Filtering
Fact tables missing dimensional columns (e.g. `category` or `channel`) are dynamically joined with `dim_product.csv` and `dim_customer.csv` on the fly via `AnalyticalDataModel.apply_scope()`.

The engine generates 8 distinct hypothesis categories:
- `DRIVER_01_INVENTORY`: Stockouts, inventory level changes.
- `DRIVER_02_PRICING`: Competitor price gap growth MoM.
- `DRIVER_03_MARKETING`: Spend increase combined with conversion rate drop MoM.
- `DRIVER_04_RETURNS`: Return rate increase MoM.
- `DRIVER_05_SUPPORT`: Support tickets, negative sentiment, and CRM/calls complaint increases MoM.
- `DRIVER_06_CUSTOMER`: Channel and customer segment mix shifts.
- `DRIVER_07_MARKET`: Target market MoM growth compared against Rest-of-Company peer growth.
- `DRIVER_08_PRODUCT_MIX`: Category share drops and mix shifts.

## 5. Scoring & Contradiction Logic

$$\text{FinalScore} = (\text{SignalScore} + \text{CorroborationScore}) \times \text{TemporalMultiplier} - \text{ContradictionPenalty}$$

- **Signal Score**: 0.0 to 6.0 based on the magnitude of the driver's own metric change. Capped at 0.0 if `supporting_evidence_count == 0`.
- **Corroboration Score**: $+3.0$ points per additional *distinct* `source_dataset`. Multiple records from the same dataset count as 1 source.
- **Temporal Multiplier**: $1.0$ if temporal alignment is `BEFORE` or `DURING`; $0.0$ if `AFTER` or `NO_CLEAR_ALIGNMENT`.
- **Contradiction Penalty**: $-15.0$ points per contradiction item.

---

## 6. Deterministic Diagnosis Gate
The `DiagnosisGate` evaluates 7 deterministic rules on the top-ranked candidate hypothesis:
1. Supporting evidence exists (`supporting_evidence_count > 0`).
2. Supporting evidence is driver-specific.
3. Temporal alignment is `BEFORE` or `DURING`.
4. Contradictory evidence does not dominate (`contradictory_evidence_count <= supporting_evidence_count` and `final_score > 0`).
5. Evidence score meets threshold ($\ge 4.0$ for `PLAUSIBLE`, $\ge 7.0$ for `STRONGLY_SUPPORTED`).
6. Evidence is relevant to requested scope.
7. Evidence is not based solely on the outcome KPI.

If all gate conditions are satisfied:
- `established_driver` = `top_hypothesis.driver`
- `overall_status` = `top_hypothesis.status`

If any gate condition fails:
- `established_driver` = `null`
- `overall_status` = `"NOT_ESTABLISHED"`
